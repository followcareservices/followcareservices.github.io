Participant & Community Advisory Panel

Have your say in how FollowCare works

FollowCare Services is a new disability support provider in Adelaide. We have applied for NDIS registration. We support people from Spanish-speaking and other culturally diverse communities.

We want people with disability to help shape how we work. We are inviting you to join our Participant & Community Advisory Panel.

I'm interested

What you would do

📄

Read some of our documents, such as our information booklet or complaints form, and tell us what is clear and what is not.

💬

Tell us what matters to you in a support provider.

💡

Give your ideas when we change our policies.

You do not need to be a FollowCare client to join.

How it works

  • Join by phone, video, in person or in writing — your choice.
  • Use any language. We can book an interpreter at no cost to you.
  • Bring a support person if you like.
  • We pay back reasonable costs, such as transport.
  • We will tell you what we changed because of your ideas.

The Panel gives advice. The Directors make the decisions and tell the Panel what they did with its advice and why. Your personal details are kept private.
